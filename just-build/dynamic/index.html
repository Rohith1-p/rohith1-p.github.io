Features

Small space. Serious utility.

Now Playing Any source

Album art, live progress, and transport controls from Music, Spotify, or Chrome. Hover and it's there.

Volume HUD Instant

A segmented glow bar that answers the instant your fingers leave the keys. No permissions, no lag.

Gestures Natural

Drag down to open, up to close, swipe to switch. Springs carry the momentum, so it never fights you.

Yours alone On-device

No accounts, no cloud, no analytics. Your Mac talks to itself and that's it.

Motion

Engineered frame by frame.

Every transition in dynamic follows one contract. The shape moves first, the content breathes in after it, and nothing ever pops.

  1. springs
    The curve family

    Open with spring(0.42, 0.8), close critically damped. The island picks its spring by where it's going, not where it's been.

  2. 40ms
    Shape leads, content follows

    The island opens first. The card reveals 40ms later, so your eye catches the geometry before the detail.

  3. stagger
    Nothing arrives at once

    Art at 0ms, text at 50ms, progress at 90ms, controls at 120ms. A cascade tighter than a blink.

  4. 60fps
    Compositor only

    Animation runs off the main thread. Your Mac can be compiling, rendering, or thinking, and the island stays glass smooth.
